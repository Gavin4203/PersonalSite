# About Me

Hi, I’m Gavin—a software development student with a minor in machine learning. I’m passionate about AI, backend development, and robotics, and I enjoy building things that solve real problems (or at least make life a bit cooler).

Right now, I’m working on a machine learning project that translates American Sign Language using computer vision and AI models. It’s been a great mix of coding, problem-solving, and learning how tech can support accessibility—something I want to explore more deeply in the future.

My dream is to build AI-driven systems and robotic assistants—yes, even robotic butlers—because I believe smart machines should be useful and fun.

When I’m not coding, I’m probably gaming, watching TV, or hanging out with my dog. I’m a bit quiet at first, but once I’m comfortable, I open up. I try to let my work speak for me—and I’m always looking to learn more.
